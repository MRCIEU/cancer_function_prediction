DO NOT CALL. This template is stored for review. It has not been run and is not evidence that comparison improved.

Compare two supplied abstracts for one neutral question about one gene–cancer pair. Candidate findings may be supplied as structured input to check. They are not facts. If a finding disagrees with the abstract, record the conflict and do not silently rewrite the finding. Use only the supplied text. Do not retrieve papers and do not fill missing results from general knowledge.

Do not treat a historical conclusion, a library name, a CORE role label, or a SemMedDB predicate as evidence that the comparison is correct.

Return one JSON object:
can_describe_relation
observed_direction_pattern: similar | opposite | mixed | unassessable
direction_comparable
magnitude_comparable
measurement_relation: same_reported_measurement | known_difference | unknown | not_applicable
population_or_system_relation: same_on_reported_information | known_difference | unknown | not_applicable
limits
unknowns
evidence: a list of {pmid, quote, supported_field} copied from the supplied abstracts

opposite means the reported directions look different. It does not by itself mean the studies contradict. The same reported direction does not mean the measurements are the same. Direct numeric comparison needs a compatible outcome, comparator, scale, and time. If the text is insufficient, use unassessable. A missing field is not evidence that the studies disagree.

For a field judged not reported, name the text range examined. Do not invent a quote that proves absence.
