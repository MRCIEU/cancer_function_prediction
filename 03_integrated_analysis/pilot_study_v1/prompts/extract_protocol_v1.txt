DO NOT CALL. This template is stored for review. It has not been run and is not evidence that extraction improved.

Read one title and abstract for one gene–cancer pair. Use only the supplied text. Return one JSON object with findings. An empty list is allowed.

A finding is one empirical result or one separate author interpretation. The same sentence may support more than one finding. Split expression and survival when one sentence reports both. Do not force one sentence to equal one finding.

For each finding include:
discourse_role: own_result | author_interpretation | background | unassessable
primary_question_type: expression_or_distribution | prognostic_association | other | unassessable
measured_or_intervened, molecular_form, compartment, comparator, outcome
result_text copied from the paper's wording, plus a structured direction that names the measured object, the comparator, and the outcome
information_status for each scientific field: reported | not_reported_in_text | unassessable | not_applicable
evidence: a list of {quote, supported_field} copied from the supplied text

Expression or distribution means the outcome is expression, localization, or abundance. Prognostic association means a marker is the predictor and the outcome is survival or another prognostic endpoint. The word expression in the predictor does not make that finding an expression question. other is kept; it is not an author interpretation.

Keep the target gene, the measured molecule and its form, the compartment, the ligand or receptor, and an intervention construct distinct. An alias, an RNA result, and a protein result are not the same object. CD137, CD137L, and CD137-Fc are not one entity.

A tumor-versus-normal increase and a high-versus-low survival difference do not share a bare increase label. Author interpretation stays outside the empirical-result comparison.
