Reassess one frozen candidate using only the original candidate, its quotes, and the newly supplied abstracts. Copy query_execution_status and retrieval_status from the input. Do not upgrade a failed or empty search into evidence that a gap is real.

Return one JSON object with:
query_execution_status
retrieval_status
new_material_relation: directly_addresses | related_different_condition | not_relevant | insufficient_information
candidate_state: refined | challenged | withdrawn | still_open_in_examined_sample | not_assessable_query_failure
reason
evidence: a list of {pmid, quote} copied from the supplied abstracts

directly_addresses requires the same gene and the candidate's question. related_different_condition also requires that gene, with a different reported condition. A paper about the disease that does not concern the gene is not_relevant. If query_execution_status is not valid, candidate_state is not_assessable_query_failure. No new abstract means the examined search did not supply material; it does not show that the literature lacks an answer.
